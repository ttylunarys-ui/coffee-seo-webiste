A practical starting point

Keep your first brew simple.

Try 20 grams of coffee with 320 grams of water as a starting point for pour-over brewing. Keep your other variables steady, then adjust one thing at a time to discover what tastes best to you.

This is a starting recipe, not a universal rule. Different beans, roast levels, and brewing methods may call for different ratios.
