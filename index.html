A note to fellow coffee lovers

You don't need to get it perfect.

Your first pour might be uneven. Your grind might be a little too coarse. Some days, the coffee will taste better than others. That's part of learning.

Start with what you have. Change one thing at a time. Pay attention to what you enjoy. The best coffee routine is one that makes sense for your everyday life.

Here's to the next good cup.
