How to Make Pour Over Coffee: A Beginner's Guide

Pour over coffee is a simple brewing method that gives you more control over the flavor of your coffee. This guide explains how beginners can make pour over coffee at home.

What Is Pour Over Coffee?

Pour over coffee is a manual brewing method in which hot water is slowly poured over ground coffee. The water passes through the coffee grounds and a paper filter before reaching the cup.

What You Need

  • Pour over coffee dripper
  • Paper coffee filter
  • Fresh coffee beans
  • Hot water
  • Coffee grinder
  • Scale

How to Make Pour Over Coffee

Step 1: Grind Your Coffee Beans

Start with freshly roasted coffee beans and grind them to a medium-fine consistency.

Step 2: Heat the Water

Heat your water and prepare your coffee dripper and paper filter.

Step 3: Add the Coffee

Place the ground coffee into the filter and gently shake the dripper to create an even coffee bed.

Step 4: Pour the Water

Slowly pour hot water over the coffee grounds in a circular motion. Continue pouring gradually until you reach your desired amount.

Common Pour Over Coffee Mistakes

Beginners often use water that is too hot, grind their coffee incorrectly, or pour the water too quickly. Consistent brewing technique can help improve the final flavor.

Final Thoughts

Making pour over coffee does not have to be complicated. With the right equipment and a little practice, beginners can develop a simple and enjoyable brewing routine.
