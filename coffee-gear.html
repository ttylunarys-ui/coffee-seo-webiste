Before You Buy

Choose What Works for You

The best coffee gear is not necessarily the most expensive. Think about how you brew, how often you make coffee, and how much maintenance you are comfortable with.

  • Your brewing routine: Choose equipment that fits the amount of time you have each day.
  • Your budget: Prioritize useful features instead of paying for extras you may never use.
  • Ease of cleaning: Removable parts and simple designs can make daily maintenance much easier.
  • Consistency: For grinders and brewers, look for features that help you repeat a recipe and get similar results.
